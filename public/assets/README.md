# Asset handoff

No supplied image or PDF was available as a local file during implementation. Do not replace these missing originals with stock or generated photography.

When AIRCON source files are provided, retain them unchanged under `originals/` and place web-ready copies in these folders:

- `brand/` - original AIRCON logo
- `company/` - building exterior and facility photographs
- `factory/` - manufacturing equipment and facility
- `automation/` - welding, assembly, fixtures and automation machinery
- `railway/` - railway equipment and systems
- `products/` - individual confirmed product photography
- `hose/` - hose assembly equipment and finished assemblies
- `customers/` - separate, approved customer logos
- `projects/` - approved project photographs

The asset inventory in `docs/asset-inventory.md` records the supplied previews, intended placements and confirmation requirements.
